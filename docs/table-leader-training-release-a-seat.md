# When a guest can't come

Plans change, and that's all right. When a guest tells you they can't make it, please release their seat. It takes less than a minute, and it helps us plan the meal and give the seat to someone else.

1. **Open your private guest link.** It's in your Table Leader confirmation email. Can't find it? Go to **leadcma.org/dinner/table** and use **Lost your link?** We'll email it to you.
2. **Find your guest** and click **Edit** next to their name.
3. **Click "Can't come · release their seat"** at the bottom.
4. **Click OK** to confirm.

Your guest moves to the bottom of your list, marked Cancelled. We're told right away, and your guest won't get any more reminders about the dinner.

**If they can come after all,** add them again from the same page.

**If you can't come yourself,** please call or email [Table Leader Coordinator name] at [phone or email]. Your own seat can't be released from your page.
