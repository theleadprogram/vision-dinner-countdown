# Vision Dinner emails – March 2027

Encouragement and follow-up emails for the LEAD Africa Vision Dinner on **Saturday, March 6, 2027, at The Woodland, Strongsville, OH**.

## Who gets what

| Layer | Who | How it's sent | When |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Confirmations** (already built) | Everyone, the moment they register | Board automations on Guests | On registration |
| **Dinner campaign:** 12 emails, G1–G12 | Everyone attending, Table Leaders included | monday campaigns, audience = Fundraising Campaigns **2027 March Vision Dinner** | Fixed dates, Nov 6 to Feb 24, about every 10 days |
| **Table Leader sequence:** 6 emails, S1–S6 | Table Leaders | Monday sequence on All Contacts | Weekly, counted from the day they're enrolled |
| **Table Leader dated emails:** 4 emails, D1–D4 | Table Leaders | Board automations on Guests | Fixed dates: training, meal count, the night, thank-you |
| **Dinner-week reminders** (already built) | Everyone | Board automations on Guests | From Mon Mar 1 |

The two kinds of timing do different jobs:
- **Date-based emails** carry news everyone needs at the same moment: what the evening holds, the meal count, the night itself. Someone who registers late starts with the next one due.
- **The sequence** carries the Table Leader coaching (make a list, pray, invite, register, follow up). Those steps are the same whenever someone joins, so they count from the day each Table Leader is enrolled.

## The dinner campaign

### Audience

Every registrant's contact gets the Fundraising Campaigns label **2027 March Vision Dinner** (plugin 1.6.0, confirmed in Monday). That includes guests, Table Leaders, every Spouse/+1, and guests a Table Leader adds. Use that label as the campaign segment.

- **Unsubscribed contacts are left alone.** The site sets Marketing Status to Marketing contact only on new contacts and on contacts where it's blank. Someone who unsubscribed won't get the campaign, but still gets their confirmation and dinner-week reminders.
- **A released seat keeps the label.** Someone who cancels would keep receiving the campaign. Remove the label by hand when a seat is released, or have the site do it (not built yet).
- **Check whether the segment is re-checked at each send.** If Campaigns fixes the audience when the campaign is created, late registrants would be missed. In that case refresh the segment before each send.

# Table Leader sequence

Six weekly coaching emails, counted from the day each Table Leader is enrolled. They cover the steps every Table Leader takes, whenever they join.

### Setting it up

- **Board:** All Contacts, where your other sequences run.
- **Enroll** each Table Leader when they register. That may be done by hand, or automatically when their VD Table Leader column changes to Yes.
- **Stop enrolling after Mon Jan 25, 2027.** The sequence runs 35 days, so anyone enrolled later would still be receiving it after the dinner. Table Leaders who join after Jan 25 get the start-here section in their confirmation, the dated emails below, and calls from the calling team.
- **No private link in these emails.** Sequences send from All Contacts, which doesn't hold the Guest link (the site stopped writing it there in version 1.4.0). The emails point Table Leaders to their confirmation email instead, and to "Lost your link?" at leadcma.org/dinner/table.
- **Watch the volume.** Table Leaders also get the dinner campaign, so some weeks they'll receive two emails. If that feels like too much, space the sequence every 10 days instead (days 2, 10, 20, 30, 40, 50), and stop enrolling after Jan 15.
