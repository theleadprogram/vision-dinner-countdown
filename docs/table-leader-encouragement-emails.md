# Table Leader encouragement emails – March 2027

Encouragement and follow-up emails for registered Table Leaders, mapped to the countdown weeks for the LEAD Africa Vision Dinner on **Saturday, March 6, 2027, at The Woodland, Strongsville, OH**.

These are separate from the emails the dinner boards already send: the Table Leader confirmation with the private guest link, the guest-link resend, and the five dinner-week reminders. This series begins after the confirmation and ends before dinner week, with a thank-you the week after.

## How to use them in Monday

- **Audience:** the Table Leaders – March 2027 board, every item whose Status isn't Stepped back. Send from clint@leadcma.org, as the other dinner emails are.
- **Send day:** Tuesday at 10 am Eastern of each countdown week. Each week's group on the countdown board ends on a Saturday, so Tuesday lands early in that week.
- **Merge fields:** `[First name]` is the Table Leaders › First name column, and `[Guest link]` is the Table Leaders › Guest link column. Everything else in [square brackets] is a fact still to confirm. Fill it in before the email is scheduled.
- **Late joiners:** the emails are tied to dates, not to the day someone said yes. A Table Leader who joins in January simply starts with that week's email. Their confirmation email and packet cover the basics.
- **The calls still matter most.** These emails support the calling team, who call every other week until 4 weeks out, then weekly. They don't replace those calls.

Thank you for saying yes to leading a table at the LEAD Africa Vision Dinner on Saturday, March 6, at The Woodland in Strongsville.

We want you to know why your table matters so much. The Vision Dinner is not a ticketed event. Every guest in the room is there because someone they trust personally invited them. That someone is you. Your table is how new friends first hear what God is doing through leaders in Africa.

## 5 · Week 8 · Tue Jan 5, 2027

**Subject:** This is the month to invite
**Preview:** Two months from Saturday. Here's how to ask.

Dear [First name],

Happy New Year. The dinner is two months from this Saturday, and January is the month to invite.

A personal invitation is the heart of the whole evening. Here is a simple way to ask:

1. **Call or meet in person.** A text or email is easy to set aside; a conversation is not.
2. **Say why it matters to you.** "LEAD is training leaders in Africa, and I've seen what it does. I'd love for you to hear about it."
3. **Be clear about the evening.** A dinner, a short program and real stories. There is no cost to attend.
4. **Ask for a yes.** "Will you join me at my table on March 6?"
5. **Register them that day.** Use your private link (more on that next week).

If someone says no, thank them warmly and move to the next name. Every yes fills a seat, and every conversation plants a seed.

You can do this,
Clint Bieri
LEAD
